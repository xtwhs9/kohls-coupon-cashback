## How do people actually get 30% off at Kohl's?

By holding a Kohl's Card — that's the primary mechanism. Kohl's reserves its steepest sitewide discounts for cardholders, running cardholder credit events a couple of times a year (September and December are the usual slots per TopCashback's own Kohl's guide) with up to 30% off, plus occasional 40% off mystery codes during bigger events. The card's full value adds up beyond the codes:

- **7.5% rewards on every purchase** as a cardholder, versus 5% for the free Kohl's Rewards program — for cardholders, Capital One converts the rewards balance into Kohl's Cash in $5 increments on the first day of the following month, valid for 30 days
- A **40% off discount code** unlocked when you register the card

The trade-off is that it's a store credit card issued through Capital One, and carrying a balance defeats the purpose quickly. A reasonable rule: it's worth opening only if you already shop at Kohl's regularly and pay the bill in full every month. ## How many Kohl's codes can you stack, and in what order?

Kohl's allows one sitewide percentage code per order — sitewide % off codes never stack with each other — but that code does combine with category coupons and shipping coupons. The total number of promo codes allowed per order varies by checkout method: long-running Kohl's deal communities report up to **4 codes on desktop** checkout and **2 on the mobile app or in-store kiosk**, though some coupon trackers cap it at 2. When in doubt, plan around two: one category code plus one sitewide code is the most reliable stack.

The order they apply isn't the order you enter them. Kohl's checkout always applies codes in this sequence: department % off codes first, then dollar-off coupons (including Kohl's Cash), and the sitewide percentage code last. Whether Kohl's Cash earns on the pre-discount or post-discount total depends on that promotion's terms — check the offer banner that day rather than assuming.

A concrete stack from the recent cycle, using example codes the deal communities tracked: a $100 men's order takes MENS10 ($10 off $50 men's) down to $90, then SAVE30 or GET20 knocks another 20–30% off the remainder. Stacking a cardholder 40% mystery code with a $10-off-$50 category coupon is also confirmed to work during cardholder events, and those orders still earn Kohl's Cash.

A few stacking traps:

- **Kohl's Cash earn and redeem are separate windows.** Cash earned October 9–18, for example, redeems October 19–25. Miss the window and it expires.
- **Kohl's Cash cannot be used at Sephora at Kohl's.** Sephora purchases get their own occasional member codes instead.
- **Standard shipping is free at $49**, and it's $8.95 below that. If you're just under, free store pickup (BOPUS) avoids shipping entirely — and through early November, picking up a $49+ order at a store earned an extra $5 Kohl's Cash after pickup, activatable at checkout.
- Clearance runs up to 70% off and is a legitimate stacking partner — clearance prices, a category code and a sitewide code on the same item is where Kohl's gets cheap.
